**Candidate 1 — `missed_component_memory` (exploitation, axes B+C):**
Adds a per-atomic-component miss counter alongside the existing confusion matrix. When a prediction omits part of a compound ground-truth (e.g. predicts "诈骗" when the target is "诈骗;组织、领导传销活动"), each missing component is tallied separately. At retrieval time, every stored example receives a `ALPHA * log(1 + sum_of_missed_counts_for_its_components)` bonus on top of its Jaccard score, so examples of chronically-missed labels rise in rank even when they're not textually similar to the query. Both confusion counts and miss counts decay after each batch. The confusion matrix in the frontier treats compound strings as opaque keys and can never express this sub-label signal.

**Candidate 2 — `recent_error_hint_memory` (exploration, axis A+E):**
Prepends a compact "Recent corrections" section above retrieved examples — a rolling deduped window of the last 10 distinct (wrong_pred → correct_gt) pairs, rendered as `✗ Predicted: X → ✓ Correct: Y`, most recent first. The retrieval and confusion-disambiguation logic is identical to the frontier; the only change is giving the model explicit meta-knowledge about its current failure patterns before it processes any examples, rather than forcing it to infer that signal from the distribution of retrieved examples.
