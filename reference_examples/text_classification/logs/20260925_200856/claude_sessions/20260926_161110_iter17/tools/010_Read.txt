3	Extends adaptive_tokenizer_confusion_memory with one structural change to the
4	learning strategy (axis E): confusion counts decay exponentially after each
5	training step, so the confusion matrix reflects the model's *current* error
6	patterns rather than its entire accumulated history.
7	
8	Problem with the baseline: confusion counts are monotonically increasing integers.
9	A label pair confused heavily early in training (when the model had few examples)
10	accumulates a large count that permanently dominates disambiguation slot selection,
11	even after the model has seen dozens of corrective examples and stopped making
12	that mistake. The disambiguation phase keeps injecting examples for a boundary
13	the model no longer needs.
14

16	DECAY_FACTOR (< 1). New error events are added with weight 1.0. Counts that decay
17	below a threshold are pruned to keep the matrix sparse. This gives recent errors
18	higher relative weight without requiring explicit timestamps.
19	
20	Retrieval algorithm is identical to adaptive_tokenizer_confusion_memory (two-phase:
21	confusion slots then similarity fill) so any delta is attributable purely to the
