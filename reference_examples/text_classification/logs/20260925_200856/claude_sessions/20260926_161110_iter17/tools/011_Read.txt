1	"""Hard-Example Buffer Memory.
2	
3	Augments the decayed_confusion_memory general pool with a structurally separate
4	bounded FIFO buffer of error examples.
5	
6	Problem with confusion-based systems: the confusion matrix is a sparse count over
7	label pairs — it records *which* labels are confused but stores no retrievable text
8	for the confused cases.  Examples of confusable inputs are sitting in the general
9	pool but are retrieved only if they happen to be similar to the current query; there
10	is no mechanism that preferentially surfaces examples that the model historically
11	mis-predicted.
12	
13	Fix: every example where was_correct=False is also appended to a bounded hard
14	buffer (max HARD_BUFFER_SIZE entries, FIFO eviction).  At predict time, score
15	hard-buffer examples against the query with adaptive Jaccard and inject the top
16	HARD_SLOTS examples that exceed HARD_SIM_THRESHOLD similarity before filling the
17	rest of the context from the general pool.  The similarity gate prevents injecting
18	unrelated error examples that would dilute context quality.
19	
20	This differs from utility_weighted_memory (which applied per-example outcome scores
21	to general-pool retrieval) because errors are held in a physically separate pool with
22	independent eviction; it differs from contrastive_error_memory (which stored explicit
23	wrong/correct pairs) because the prompt structure is unchanged — only the retrieval
24	source differs.
